1. The Human Reality & Grassroots Crisis

In remote villages across Jharkhand, communities encounter severe grassroots challenges: toxic fluoride in Palamu water ($> 4.5\text{ mg/L}$), neck-blast fungal outbreaks on Gumla paddy crops, and heavy coal-dust settling over Dhanbad.

Traditionally, three separate worlds remained isolated:
1. Citizens who experienced the problems daily had no direct line to scientists or funding.
2. Universities (BIT Mesra, IIT ISM Dhanbad) had 100,000+ engineering students working on textbook projects without real-world community impact.
3. Industries (SAIL Bokaro, Tata Steel) had billions in mandatory CSR funds but lacked ground data.

SamasyaSetu (समस्या सेतु) bridges this gap, operationalizing the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) to convert grassroots grievances into structured university research projects co-funded by industry and verified by the government.

4. SIH Defense Masterclass: Top Winning Defenses

Q1: Why did you build an on-device embedding classifier instead of OpenAI GPT-4 API?
Winning Defense: (1) Zero Recurring SaaS Costs: Running OpenAI APIs for thousands of complaints would drain state funds; our local model runs 100% free on the host CPU. (2) Data Sovereignty: Citizen grievance narratives and exact GPS coordinates remain strictly on state servers. (3) Guaranteed Uptime: Third-party APIs face rate limits and outages; our local model computes embeddings in sub-15ms with 100% offline resilience.
Q2: How does priority scoring prevent urban areas from dominating rural villages?
Winning Defense: We apply a base-10 logarithm (&log;10) to the population affected. This ensures an urgent crisis in a small village of 500 people receives substantial points without being mathematically crushed by an urban complaint from 100,000 residents.
Q3: How do you verify that a problem is genuinely solved on the ground?
Winning Defense: A ticket cannot be closed by a status update alone. Resolution requires post-implementation photographs, a technical outcome summary, and verification by the administrative nodal officer, generating an official state-sealed "Before & After" Proof of Resolution Certificate.
Q4: Why should corporate giants like Tata Steel, SAIL, or ECL use SamasyaSetu for CSR?
Winning Defense: Indian companies face strict legal mandates under Section 135 of the Companies Act to deploy 2% of profits into verifiable CSR. SamasyaSetu provides a transparent dashboard tracking exact capital disbursement, milestone progress, and verified societal impact.
